# TreeScore boundary assumptions — Juhu v0.3 correction

This note supplements `boundary-assumptions-v0.2.md`. Andheri West Core and
Powai Lake + Urban Area are unchanged at v0.2.

## Juhu (`juhu`, boundary `juhu-v0.3`)

**Area:** approximately 1.90 km², down from approximately 2.85 km² in v0.2.

**Retained**

- the v0.2 northern edge below Andheri West Core
- central Juhu and Juhu Scheme-style residential fabric
- the main eastern residential edge
- the western coastal residential strip
- a conservative coastal line east of visible beach and surf

**Removed**

- the lagoon and water-adjacent transition in the southern lobe
- aerodrome or airport operational/open ground
- runway-side paved and bare surfaces
- airport-adjacent fabric outside the intended TreeScore Juhu scope
- excess beach/sea and the far southern reach toward Santacruz

The new southern edge turns west near 19.103–19.104 along the visible northern
margin of the lagoon/aerodrome complex. A narrow concave continuation retains
the established coastal residential strip to approximately 19.0965 without
wrapping around the lagoon or airport land.

The polygon has one closed outer ring and no holes. A concave outer edge is used
because the excluded lagoon/aerodrome complex connects to the discarded
southern area; a hole would falsely imply that all surrounding southern land is
part of the intended locality.

**Confidence:** `medium`. Satellite review resolved the material v0.2 scope
error, but the polygon is not an official or surveyed boundary.
